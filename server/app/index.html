Multimodal Analysis Pipeline

How DocShield Analyzes Academic Documents

Rather than relying on a single fallible indicator, DocShield aggregates multiple independent forensics checks into an explainable decision.

01

Secure Upload & Validation

MIME, magic header bytes, and SHA-256 cryptographic digest verified before storage.

02

PDF & Page Preprocessing

Multi-page PDF rendering via PyMuPDF at 200+ DPI with adaptive grayscale normalization.

03

OCR & Academic Field Extraction

Extracts student name, institution, roll number, degree, date, and grade/CGPA with coordinates.

04

Computer Vision & ELA Forensics

Error Level Analysis (ELA), Laplacian noise inconsistency index, and text baseline alignment.

05

Supervised ML Risk Engine

Classifies multi-signal 18-dim feature vector and aggregates into calibrated risk tiers.

06

Audit-Ready PDF Report

Generates formal ReportLab verification report with findings, limitations, and review recommendation.

Scope & Transparency Principles

Honest Verification, Transparent Limitations

Our system strictly distinguishes AI-assisted integrity assessment from officialissuer-backed legal verification. We never claim visual analysis alone proves a document legally authentic.

  • Initial Domain: Academic degrees, mark sheets, and provisional university diplomas (PDF, JPG, PNG).
  • No Fake Metrics: Grounded risk calculations derived from actual extracted features and validated models.
  • Human Review Workflow: Flags high-risk cases for manual inspection with highlighted anomaly coordinates.

Academic Disclaimer

Error Level Analysis, compression discrepancies, and font variances can occasionally be introduced by scanner hardware, re-saving, or PDF compilation. Findings should be treated as investigatory indicators to prioritize manual review.
